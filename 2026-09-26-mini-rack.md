My home lab hardware now lives in a small 10-inch rack that I printed on an A1 mini.
Every part fits on the 180 mm bed. The long pieces are split and joined with dovetails
or jigsaw joints.

![EliteDesk mini rack](models/mini-rack.glb)

The model above is built from the actual print files, not redrawn. Drag to orbit, use
the explode slider to pull it apart, and tap a part to see what it is.

## The frame

The frame is 270 × 200 mm and 140 mm tall. Each side is a stack of four printed
segments: a bottom rail, two 1U wall sections and a top rail. Each segment sits 2 mm
into the one below, and grey connectors bolt across every joint.

Grey cross panels close the front and back, top and bottom. Every hole in the frame
takes an M5 cage nut on the standard 236.5 mm 10-inch spacing, so normal rack gear fits.

Violet clips grip the inside corners of the frame: caps on top and feet underneath.

## The EliteDesk bay

The lower bay holds an HP EliteDesk 705 G4 Mini in a three-piece sleeve. The two ears and
the centre sleeve join with dovetails, so the whole bracket prints on one plate. Two
supports screw in from the back to hold the rear of the PC. The upper bay has the same
sleeve, empty for now.

## The top

The top is two vented covers, each printed as two halves that lock together with a
jigsaw joint. Their corners are cut to wrap around the corner caps.
