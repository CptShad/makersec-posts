Hello! I'm Varun. I'm a software engineer, and pretend to be a maker in my spare time. 
I am a hobbyist who likes to dable in Electronics and 3d Printing from time to time.
I am also learning about Systems and Networking, Which is what i mostly setup my homelab to learn.
The name **MakerSec** is an abbreviation of "Maker Sector", Which is inspired from DeadSec in Watch Dogs.

## What this is for

I wanted a maker space of my own, and for now that's this site. It's where I put what
I'm working on. Sometimes that's code, but more often it's something I've built:
electronics, a networking project like the home lab, an ESP32 build, a drone, etc.

Further out I'd like MakerSec to be an actual maker garage, a place other people can
come and build things in. I can't do that yet, so I'm starting with this.
